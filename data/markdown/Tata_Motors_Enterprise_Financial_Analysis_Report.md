## **Executive Summary** 

Tata Motors delivered a strong FY2025 at the consolidated group level. Revenue from operations was ₹4,39,695 crore, up about 1.3% from FY2024. Operating profit was ₹65,848 crore and operating cash flow was ₹63,102 crore. Profit for the year was ₹28,149 crore, although it declined from ₹31,807 crore in FY2024. The group also reported a strong balance sheet, with total assets of ₹3,78,642 crore and total liabilities of ₹2,55,888 crore at March 31, 2025. 

The financial picture shows strong operating cash generation and improved finance costs, supported by lower debt. At the same time, the decline in reported profit highlights pressure from taxes, exceptional items and business mix. The consolidated figures include Tata Motors' automotive operations and Jaguar Land Rover (JLR), so they should be used consistently when comparing enterprise-level performance. 

Data basis: Tata Motors Integrated Annual Report 2024–25 and official FY25 consolidated results. All amounts are ₹ crore.

## **Profitability Interpretation** 

Revenue grew modestly in FY2025, while total expenses were almost flat. Operating profit declined to ₹65,848 crore, but profit before tax increased to ₹33,780 crore because of lower finance costs and other factors. Profit for the year declined to ₹28,149 crore, mainly reflecting the overall effect of tax and other below-operating items.

## **Operating Cash Flow** 

Cash generated from operating activities was ₹63,102 crore in FY2025 versus ₹67,915 crore in FY2024. Although operating cash flow declined, it remained strong relative to reported profit, demonstrating the cash-generating ability of the automotive business. The company also reported automotive free cash flow of ₹22,400 crore for FY2025.

## **Growth Opportunities** 

Tata Motors can continue to grow through premium vehicles, SUVs, commercial vehicles, EVs, connected technologies and JLR's global premium portfolio. Improving domestic passenger-vehicle volumes, expanding EV adoption and maintaining commercial-vehicle profitability are important opportunities. 

## **Risks & Challenges** 

Key risks include global economic conditions, foreign-exchange movements, commodity prices, intense automotive competition, EV transition costs, regulatory changes, tariffs and geopolitical uncertainty. JLR's global exposure also means that international demand and trade conditions can materially affect consolidated results. 

## **Conclusion** 

Tata Motors entered FY2025 from a position of strong operating scale and cash generation. Revenue reached a record ₹4.40 lakh crore, while operating cash flow remained above ₹63,000 crore. The main financial strength is the combination of operating scale, cash generation and lower finance costs. The main area to monitor is the conversion of operating performance into sustainable net-profit growth, particularly amid global automotive uncertainty.
